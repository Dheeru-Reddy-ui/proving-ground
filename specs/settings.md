# Settings

Settings let the player adjust sound and erase their progress.

- SET-1: Settings can be opened from the main menu.
- SET-2: Settings has separate sliders for master volume, music volume and sound-effects volume.
- SET-3: Moving a slider changes that volume straight away.
- SET-4: Volume settings are kept after closing the settings screen.
- SET-5: Choosing to delete data asks for confirmation before anything is deleted.
- SET-6: Declining the confirmation leaves all progress unchanged.
- SET-7: Confirming deletes all progress and starts a new game: zero coins and premium, only Trash Cat and the Day theme owned, and new missions.
