# Store

The store is where players spend coins and premium currency on power-ups, characters, accessories and themes.

- STORE-1: The store can be opened from the main menu and from the game-over screen.
- STORE-2: The store always shows the player's current coin and premium balances.
- STORE-3: The store has four sections: power-ups, characters, accessories and themes.
- STORE-4: Every item shows its coin price, and its premium price when it has one.
- STORE-5: Buying an item lowers the coin balance by exactly the item's coin price and the premium balance by exactly its premium price.
- STORE-6: Buying a power-up adds one of it to the player's inventory.
- STORE-7: Buying a character, accessory or theme makes it owned, and it becomes available to select outside the store.
- STORE-8: An item the player cannot afford cannot be bought, and the price the player cannot cover is highlighted.
- STORE-9: A character or theme the player already owns is marked "Owned" and cannot be bought again.
- STORE-10: Every successful purchase both charges the price and grants the item, in a single step.
- STORE-11: Closing the store returns the player to the screen they opened it from, showing the updated balances.
