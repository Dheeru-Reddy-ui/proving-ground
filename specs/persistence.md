# Persistence

Progress survives closing and reopening the game.

- PERSIST-1: Coin and premium balances are the same after the game is closed and reopened.
- PERSIST-2: Owned characters, accessories, themes and power-ups are the same after the game is closed and reopened.
- PERSIST-3: The selected character, accessory and theme are the same after the game is closed and reopened.
- PERSIST-4: Active missions, their progress and claimed rewards are the same after the game is closed and reopened.
- PERSIST-5: Volume settings are the same after the game is closed and reopened.
- PERSIST-6: Once the tutorial is completed, it is not shown again after the game is reopened.
- PERSIST-7: High scores are kept after the game is closed and reopened.
- PERSIST-8: A fresh install, or an install with its data cleared, starts a new game: zero coins and premium, Trash Cat and the Day theme owned, and the tutorial shown again.
