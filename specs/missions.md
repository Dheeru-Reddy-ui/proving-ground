# Missions

Missions give players goals across runs and reward them with premium currency.

- MISSION-1: The player always has two active missions.
- MISSION-2: Each mission shows its objective and its premium reward.
- MISSION-3: Progress on a mission advances when the player does what the objective asks during runs.
- MISSION-4: A completed mission can be claimed from the missions screen.
- MISSION-5: Claiming a mission adds exactly its reward to the player's premium balance.
- MISSION-6: A claimed mission is replaced by a new one, so there are two active missions again.
- MISSION-7: A mission can be claimed only once.
- MISSION-8: A mission that is not complete cannot be claimed. [confirm]
